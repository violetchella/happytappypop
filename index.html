Happy Tappy Pop

How to play

  • 🎯Pick a level: Easy 🐢, Medium 🐇 or Hard 🐆.
  • ⭕Look at the big circle at the top. It shows what to find: a color or a shape.
  • 👆Tap the bubbles that match as they float up. Wrong ones just wobble, so no worries!
  • ✨At the start of each round, the right bubbles glow to help you.
  • 🍫Every right tap puts a chocolate in the fox's tray. 5 chocolates = a 🍭 lollipop sticker, and you get a new thing to find.
  • ⚡Get 3 right in a row and the bubbles go faster. Miss twice and they slow down.
  • 🎉Collect 5 lollipops for a big party, then pick a level again!
Easy: big, slow bubbles.
Medium: smaller, faster bubbles that sway.
Hard: the fastest bubbles, and sometimes you must match the color and the shape together.
